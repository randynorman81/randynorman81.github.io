Social Circle High School Exit Tickets

for teachers, no coding required

Turn any lesson into a self-grading exit ticket

Paste in your notes, slides, or a worksheet (or upload a .txt, .pdf, or .docx file) and this tool will use AI to write a short exit ticket — a few multiple-choice questions and an optional short-answer reflection — based on exactly what you taught that day.

Upload your class roster, share one link with students, and watch responses get scored automatically as they come in.

Create an Exit Ticket →

How it works

  1. Upload or paste your lesson content. The AI reads it and drafts questions — you can edit anything before publishing.
  2. Upload your roster. A CSV or a simple list of student names, so students just pick their name instead of typing it.
  3. Share the student link. Students answer on their phone or laptop and submit.
  4. Check the results page. Multiple-choice is graded instantly; short answers get a quick AI-assisted check that you can override with one click.

Your data

Exit tickets are stored on this site's own Netlify deployment (Netlify Blobs) — nothing is sent anywhere else except the lesson text, which is sent to the AI provider once to generate questions. Each exit ticket you create has its own private results link; keep it somewhere safe, since it's the only way to see or edit results later.
